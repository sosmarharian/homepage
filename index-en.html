We could start with numbers — 30+ weddings, professional gear, years behind the camera. And we're proud of it. But that's not what matters on your day.

How We Work

1. GET IN TOUCH

Drop us a line via the form or WhatsApp — we'll be back to you within a day. From there we set up a relaxed call: we listen to your story, get a sense of your day, and figure out together what fits.

2. OFFER & BOOKING

If it feels right on both sides, we put together a tailored offer. A signed contract and a small deposit hold your date in our calendar — the remainder is due only after the wedding.

3. PLANNING TOGETHER

In the weeks leading up to the day we stay in touch — about the flow, the locations, the moments you care about most. Just before the wedding we sit down once more and walk through the timeline, so nothing feels rushed when it matters.

4. YOUR WEDDING DAY

We arrive early, often for the getting-ready, and stay with you through the day — calm and quietly present, never staging anything. As a duo who works in sync, we catch the glances and the unguarded moments between you without missing each other's frame.

5. ENGAGEMENT SHOOT (OPTIONAL)

Anyone who wants comes by for a short shoot beforehand — a chance to get comfortable in front of the camera before it actually counts. Most of our couples tell us afterwards that it made the day itself far more relaxed.

6. YOUR GALLERY

Within 4–6 weeks you receive your private online gallery to view, share, and download. For films we include a feedback round, until the edit sounds and feels exactly the way you imagined it.
